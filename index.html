02 / Recommendation

Start with a Marketing Intelligence and Reactivation pilot.

The first project should be commercially visible, measurable quickly, and low-risk from a clinical data perspective. Marketing and reactivation sits in the right place: spend, speed and conversion.

Why this is the right wedge

  • It avoids starting with sensitive patient or clinical data.
  • It targets a known cost centre: Meta, Google, creative performance and lead handling.
  • It creates a measurable proof point before asking the business to trust broader automation.
  • It leaves behind reusable infrastructure: prompts, logs, approvals, data rules and reporting patterns.

What should not happen first

  • Do not begin with a broad company chatbot.
  • Do not let each department keep building isolated prompt libraries and vendor experiments.
  • Do not start with clinical-data automation before trust, governance and approval rules are clear.
  • Do not accept hidden token margins, unclear tool ownership or vague AI magic delivery.
Recommended first moveReview Robbie's internal AI audit spreadsheet, interview the relevant department owners, identify the best pilot, then return with a fixed audit scope and practical implementation map.
03 / Workflows

Initial workflows with clear business value.

These are not generic AI ideas. They are the first workflows implied by the call: visible value, clear approval gates, and a path to scale once the pilot proves itself.

Marketing control room

MetaGoogleCreative

Pull campaign performance into a daily AI review, flag wasted spend or creative fatigue, draft recommendations, and route changes for human approval.

Impact: Faster correction cycles, less budget leakage and clearer creative testing.

Lead response and reactivation

Lead formsBookingsSMS

Segment unconverted leads and lapsed patients, draft compliant outreach, trigger approved SMS/email/call tasks, and measure bookings by clinic.

Impact: More bookings from existing demand without manual list chasing.

Call handling economics

InboundOutboundVoice cost

Compare current outbound AI and inbound call tools against owned alternatives across conversion, cost per minute and handoff quality.

Impact: A practical keep, replace, rebuild or integrate decision.

Head-office knowledge layer

SOPsPromptsDecisions

Create a common structure for approved prompts, operating notes, department context and reusable workflows.

Impact: Less reinvention and a cleaner path for leaders to use AI consistently.

Department workflow map

FinanceOpsProperty

Use the existing internal audit, then interview key owners to map repeatable work, systems touched, risk level and likely ROI.

Impact: A serious implementation queue instead of an endless brainstorm.

Governed rollout playbook

ApprovalsLogsScale

Define where AI can act, where it can only recommend, what gets logged, who approves changes and how rollout happens.

Impact: The operating difference between clever tools and a scalable system.
