Robbie has already done the hard internal first step: asking teams what is repetitive, rule-based and automatable. The gap is a company-owned structure that keeps that intelligence usable across head office without exposing sensitive clinical data too early.

02 / Diagnosis

WhiteHorse should solve the operating model, not just build a tool.

The proposal should meet Robbie where he is: sophisticated, cautious about vendors, aware of token economics, and sensitive to staff trust. The answer is a governed implementation path that Wellbeing can own.

What Wellbeing365 already has

  • An internal automation audit with real department input.
  • Leaders who understand the repeatable work in each function.
  • Active AI experimentation across head office.
  • Commercial pressure points in marketing, lead handling and call economics.

What needs to be controlled

  • Clinical and patient data boundaries.
  • Fragmented prompts, tools and vendor-managed automations.
  • Staff fear that automation is being used to replace roles.
  • Hidden token margin, unclear ownership and black-box systems.
03 / Recommended pilot

Start where spend, speed and conversion meet.

Marketing Intelligence and Reactivation is the right first proof point. It avoids clinical data, touches revenue, has measurable inputs and outputs, and gives head office a repeatable governance pattern before broader rollout.

Why this first

It is commercially visible.

Paid media, lead forms, conversion, lapsed patients and call workflows produce numbers quickly. The pilot can prove or disprove value without a long transformation program.

Why it is safer

It avoids the clinical core.

The first build should operate around marketing and head-office data, with human approval before outbound messages or campaign changes go live.

Why it scales

It creates the rollout pattern.

The pilot establishes logging, approvals, cost visibility, data boundaries and ownership rules that can be reused across finance, operations and property.

05 / Governance

The trust layer is part of the product.

For Wellbeing365, the implementation is only valuable if it creates confidence. The system needs clear ownership, transparent usage costs, visible logs and approval gates.

Owned by Wellbeing

Workflows, prompts, data mappings and documentation should be portable. The audit should contain enough implementation detail for WhiteHorse, an internal team or another provider to build from.

Approval-gated by default

AI can draft, rank, flag and recommend. Campaign changes, patient communications and sensitive actions should require human approval until trust is earned.

Transparent on cost

Token usage, hosting, vendor fees and implementation fees should be visible. No hidden margin inside usage and no upside clipping as a default commercial model.

06 / Business fit

A modern clinic network needs a modern operating spine.

The public Wellbeing brand is human, clinical and multi-location. The AI program should support the system around care: marketing, bookings, follow-up, reporting, knowledge and operations.
